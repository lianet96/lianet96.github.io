About My Work

✦

My work lives between softness and tension — a reflection of identity, intimacy, and memory through film, illustration, and installation. I’m drawn to the moments when beauty turns unsettling and vulnerability becomes power. Every piece I make is a way to understand the strange poetry of being alive.
